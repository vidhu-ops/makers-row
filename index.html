About Makers' Row

One marketplace, three ways to get design work done

Makers' Row exists because "make it yourself" and "hire someone" shouldn't be two completely different products. Most people need something in between: a good starting point they can bend to their needs, made by someone who's good at this, at a price that doesn't require a full agency retainer.

So the marketplace works three ways. You can buy a finished template, graphic or AI-service pack outright and download it immediately. You can open anything — bought or not yet bought — in the built-in editor and rework it yourself: swap text, recolor, drop in a new photo, resize, then add your version to the cart. Or, if you'd rather not touch a single layer, you can describe what you need on the Bespoke tab and have a designer build it around your brief, delivered to your email within the day.

For buyers

Browsing doesn't require an account. Filter by Templates, Graphics & Stock, or AI Services, or search directly. Every listing shows the seller, the price, and whether it's ready to download or worth customizing first. Add as many items as you like — off-the-shelf purchases, edited designs, and bespoke requests all sit in the same cart, and checkout is a single step.

For sellers

The Sell tab is open to anyone with something worth listing: a template pack, an icon set, stock photography, or a bundle of AI-generated assets. Set your title, category, price and a preview image, and it's live in the marketplace immediately, ready for buyers to purchase or customize.

The editor

The in-browser editor covers the core of what you'd expect from a design tool: text and headings, shapes, your own uploaded images, a stock-photo search panel, color and font controls, layering, undo/redo, zoom, and a one-click PNG export. It's built for quick, practical edits — not for replacing a full design suite, but for getting a template from "close enough" to "exactly right" without leaving the page.

Frequently asked

How fast is a bespoke request, really?

Your finished creative is delivered within the day for a typical graphic, logo concept, or short deck. More involved briefs (full brand kits, multi-page decks) take a note in the confirmation email with a realistic estimate.

Can I edit something before I buy it?

Yes. Opening a marketplace listing in the editor doesn't charge you — you only pay when you add the finished result to your cart and check out.

Where do the stock photos come from?

In this demo, image search returns placeholder photos so you can see how the panel works. A live version would connect to a licensed stock/image API for real, rights-cleared results.

What do I get after checkout?

Marketplace purchases and anything you've built in the editor download right away. Bespoke requests are delivered to the email (or contact) you provide within the day.
